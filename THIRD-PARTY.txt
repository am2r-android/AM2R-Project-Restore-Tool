AM2R Project Restore Tool

The verified-original and binary-patch workflow derives from the AM2R Android
Autopatcher under its MIT license. The copyright and permission notice is in
LICENSE. This tool is separate from its Android application.
https://github.com/am2r-android/AM2R-Autopatcher-Android

xdelta3 is a decoder/encoder dependency by Joshua MacDonald. The portable
application includes xdelta3 3.2.2 under Apache License 2.0. The standalone
Python source can also use a separately installed decoder. The portable archive
includes the matching notices in licenses/.
https://github.com/jmacd/xdelta

Portable applications also include Python, Tcl/Tk and supporting libraries.
Their original notices are in licenses/. PyInstaller supplies the executable
loader under its license and application-bundling exception. BUILD-INFO.json
records the build versions; restorer-source.zip contains this utility's source.
https://www.python.org/
https://www.tcl-lang.org/
https://pyinstaller.org/

GMXDataSync by YellowAfterlife supplies the historical source-restoration model.
Its implementation is not included or copied here. It targets legacy GMX projects;
the supplied projects use modern GameMaker YYP definitions and versioned manifests.
https://github.com/YAL-GameMaker-Tools/GmxDataSync
